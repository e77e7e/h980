FLIES OF THE HOUSE

greenbottle fly

rarity: common

habitat: worldwide pest

what they like: decaying organic matter. You've also probably have seen them buzzing around trash or poop

life cycle: real quick. 10 to 25 days. that is approximately 20 life cycles in a year. spoiler: they can lay up to 1200 eggs in their lifetime.
